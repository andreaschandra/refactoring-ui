Frequently asked questions

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Email sales@tasklane.example and we’ll reply within one business day.

How does the free trial work?

Pro and Team both include a 14-day trial with every feature unlocked. No credit card is needed to start. When the trial ends you can pick a paid plan or drop back to Free without losing any of your tasks.

Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?

Yes. Switching to annual takes effect right away and we credit the unused part of your current month. Switching to monthly takes effect at the end of your annual term.

How are users counted?

You pay for each member of your workspace. Guests, such as clients you invite to a single project, are free and don’t count toward your seats.

What happens to my data if I downgrade?

Nothing is deleted. Projects stay intact, but paid features like timelines and automations become read-only until you upgrade again.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?

Yes. Registered nonprofits and accredited schools get 50% off Pro and Team. Email us from your organization’s address to apply.

Which payment methods do you accept?

All major credit cards on every paid plan. Annual Team plans can also pay by invoice with net-30 terms.
