Questions, answered

Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?

Yes. Switch any time from your billing settings. Moving to annual takes effect right away and we credit the unused part of your current month. Moving to monthly starts when your annual term ends.

What happens when I hit the Free plan limits?

Nothing gets deleted. You can keep working in your existing projects, but you'll need to archive one or upgrade before creating a fourth project or inviting a sixth member.

Who counts as a user?

Anyone who can sign in to your workspace. Guests you invite to view or comment on a single project are free and don't count toward your seats.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for, then your workspace moves to Free. Your data stays put, and you can export it whenever you like.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or schools?

Yes. Registered nonprofits and accredited schools get 50% off Pro and Team. Email us from your organization's address and we'll set it up.

Which payment methods do you accept?

All major credit and debit cards. Annual Team plans can also pay by invoice with bank transfer.
