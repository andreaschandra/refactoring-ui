Frequently asked questions

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Contact our team.

Is the Free plan really free?

Yes. The Free plan has no time limit and doesn’t require a credit card. It includes unlimited projects and tasks for up to 5 members.

How does the 14-day trial work?

You get full access to Pro or Team for 14 days with no card required. When the trial ends, you can pick a plan or your workspace moves to Free automatically — nothing is deleted.

How is per-user pricing calculated?

You pay for each member in your workspace. Guests — clients or contractors you invite to specific projects — are free and don’t count toward your seat total.

Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?

Anytime. Switching to annual applies the 20% discount immediately, and we credit the unused part of your current month. Switching to monthly takes effect at your next renewal.

What happens when I add or remove members?

New members are prorated for the rest of your billing period. Removed seats are credited toward your next invoice.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?

Yes — eligible nonprofits and accredited schools get 50% off Pro and Team. Contact us with proof of status to apply.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your billing settings and you’ll keep paid features until the end of the current period. You can export all your data as CSV or JSON at any time.
